### F1 tradeoff (needs your decision)

Hard share caps cannot be guaranteed with the current dollar-sized FAK. The smallest change that would guarantee them is a share-sized FAK limit order: `create_order(OrderArgs(size=target, price=cap), FAK)`, so `takerAmount` = shares and `makerAmount` = shares × cap.

- **What it gives:** shares are at most the target and spend is at most target × cap. Price improvement then saves dollars instead of buying more shares.
- **What it costs:** new order construction and signing amounts in the live sender, a new paper fill model, and a different P/L profile. The venue's FAK limit-order behaviour needs a live check.
- **Today:** the dollar caps are hard. `makerAmount` equals the stake exactly (tested), and the canary spend cap and cash checks are enforced on dollars. Measured overshoot: 10 shares at a $0.50 decision filled 10.20 / 12.50 / 16.67 shares when the ask fell to 0.49 / 0.40 / 0.30, spending exactly $5.00 each time.

### P2 paper model limitation (not changed; your decision)

In the short live run, 10 of 12 sniper paper orders were refused with *unknown book state at the modelled arrival*.

The paper broker fills against the book at the modelled match instant on the exchange clock. It waits `SNIPER_BOOK_WAIT_MS` (500 ms) for a feed message stamped after that instant. In those cases the feed's newest message was 0.05–4.6 s older than the instant: a quiet book late in the round, which the rule cannot tell from a lagging feed.

This never fakes a fill, but it understates the paper fill rate. `SNIPER_BOOK_CLOCK=local` (the book in our hands at that instant) is the existing alternative. It changes how paper fills are modelled, so it was not changed.

### Run it on the VPS

```bash

.venv/bin/python paper_realtime.py run --markets 100 --dir /opt/sniperbtc-realtime
```

- **Progress and alerts, any time:** `tail -f /opt/sniperbtc-realtime/monitor.jsonl`
